# CRM Analytics and Tableau

**Detected by** `wave*` metadata (applications, dashboards, dataflows, recipes, datasets) or namespace `wave`; Tableau shows as CSP trusted sites or embedded LWC.

## Document

| Chapter | What |
|---|---|
| 11 Reporting | `reporting.analytics` section: apps and their audience, data preparation (dataflows or recipes, schedules, sources), datasets, dashboards embedded in Lightning pages, security predicates and sharing inheritance |
| 9 Integrations | External data sources and connectors |
| 10 Security | Analytics permission sets and licences, row-level security |

## Read

`waveApplications`, `waveDashboards`, `waveDataflows`, `waveRecipes`, `waveDatasets` (XMD), flexipages embedding analytics components.

## Watch out

Lightning reports and dashboards (chapter 11 first section) and CRM Analytics are distinct: document both when both exist.
